# BF4 family screen on the fused-step route (2026-09-24)

This is a diagnostic screen. It admits no family, and production precision is
unchanged. The owner approved evaluating BF4 against an accuracy floor instead
of exact greedy parity (see `serve-qwen38-fused-gdn-decode-step`). The floor
below was fixed before any BF4 run.

## Setup

- P150x2 devices `0` and `1`, B=1, `tt-metal-0.77.0`, and GDN decode mode `step`.
  The checkpoint is `1d4bf0f2ff6012fd82039f2fa52739d0dd7c60c0`.
- Each family ran in a separate process with only its gate set:
  `QWEN36_GDN_BF4`, `QWEN36_MLP_DOWN_BF4`, or `QWEN36_LM_HEAD_BF4`. The `.bfp4`
  caches predate this screen (2026-08-25). No run wrote a BFP8 bin.
- The reference is the BFP8 fused-step run: 42.56 ms replay and mean passage NLL
  0.7451.
- Corpus: 8 prompts × 96 teacher-forced positions, fed the reference greedy
  tokens and compared on the reference top-64 support, plus 6 passages scored
  by NLL: prose, Python, history, recipe, calculus, and SQL.
- The throwaway probe is `.pi/tmp/gdn-step/bf4_eval.py`. The raw JSON is in
  `.pi/tmp/gdn-step/bf4-*.json`.
- Declared floor: mean passage NLL increase ≤ 0.010 nats and mean teacher-forced
  top-1 agreement ≥ 0.97.

Per-passage NLL for GDN BF4 was +0.050 prose, −0.017 Python, +0.028 history,
+0.015 recipe, −0.007 calculus, and −0.022 SQL. For comparison, the step route
moved passage NLL by −0.005 to +0.000 against the composite route.

## Verdict and non-claims

- MLP-down BF4 fails the declared floor.
- LM-head BF4 passes the floor. It saves only 0.3 ms per token (0.7 percent)
  against a predicted 0.9 ms, so its cost/benefit does not justify a production
  change.
- GDN-projection BF4 passes the floor and saves 2.5 ms per token (5.9 percent).
  On two of eight prompts (Fibonacci, train speed), greedy decode skips the
  `<think>` block at token 1 where the reference opens one. That behaviour
  change is outside what the floor measures. It is left for owner review and
  stays default-off.
- This is a single-session screen without matched order-balanced roots. It makes
  no long-context or sampled-serving claim.
